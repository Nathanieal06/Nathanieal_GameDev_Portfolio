Greetings, I'm Nathanieal Sandipurti, a self-taught game programmer and Unity developer. To me, games are like movies except the audience gets to grab the wheel. Instead of just watching a story unfold, you get to control the protagonist's choices and rewrite their destiny with every decision. I've sharpened my skills through 3 game jams, built 10+ Unity projects, and published 3 games so far. Each one has taught me something new about mechanics, storytelling, and what makes a player actually feel the choices they make. Right now, I'm working toward my biggest goal yet: creating a full choice-based story game one where every decision genuinely branches the narrative and makes players feel like the author of their own journey.
